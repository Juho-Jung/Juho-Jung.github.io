Juho Jung

I am a Research Scientist (Alternative Military Service) at VUNO Inc. My research focuses on uncertainty aware multimodal LLMs adaptation in medical image and improving uncertainty quantification of LLMs and hallucinations in real world environments.

Before joining VUNO I obtained my Master's from the Graduate School of Applied Artificial Intelligence at SKKU (Full Academic Excellence Scholarship), under the supervision of Prof. Jinyoung Han, where I researched uncertainty-aware Multitask Learning in Medical image and Multimodal Feature Alignment in complex and unknown modality. I completed my bachelor's degree (Summa Cum Laude) in Applied Artificial Intelligence at the SKKU. I had the privilege of working with Zhi-Qi Cheng, Alexander Hauptmann, and David Mortensen as a Visiting Scholar in the School of Computer Science Intensive Program in AI at Carnegie Mellon University.

Research Interests

I am interested in advancing uncertainty-aware multimodal learning for medical imaging. My work explores how to adapt large vision-language models to clinical data, quantify and calibrate their uncertainty, and mitigate hallucinations in real-world medical settings. My current research emphasizes:

• Vision-Language Models for medical imaging
• Uncertainty Quantification in multimodal learning
• Hallucination detection and mitigation
• Representation Learning for medical images

Deconstructing Multi-Task Active Learning: The Paradox of Gradient Conflict and Orthogonal Decomposition
Juho Jung*, Seongjae Kang*, Jinyoung Han
NeurIPS (Poster), 2026
project page arXiv paper code

Gradient conflict leaves the acquisition score and is resolved by the optimizer; selection becomes two ordered stages that filter by informativeness, then maximize coverage among the survivors.

SPARCL: Spatially Adaptive Regional Coverage for Active Learning in Chest X-ray Analysis
Juho Jung, Seongjae Kang, Mijoo Kim, Sungduk Cho, Yunkyoung Jun, Sunghoon Joo, Yoona Hwang
arXiv preprint, 2026
arXiv paper code

An entropy-gated acquisition strategy that balances predictive uncertainty with within-batch spatial coverage, together with spatial-reliability metrics that expose regional gaps hidden by aggregate Dice.
